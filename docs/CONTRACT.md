## V1.3 — carried rifle, split and rivals
`hero-pose.js` exports pure `computeHeroPose(s)` with world-space body/arm/leg anchors, both weapon grips and the muzzle. Engine shots and HeroArt share this geometry. `s.aimX/aimY/aimSide` persist between shots independently of movement. `s.stunt={kind:'split',active,progress,time,duration,faceX,faceY,count}` controls a1.3s cosmetic fullsplit; actual running charges its cadence. Modal modes freeze it. HeroArt uses an arm-free modular torso atlas; image failure retains the same procedural held-rifle rig.

Enemy types also include `rival_goat` and `rival_rooster`, both `sex:'male'`; at most two active. Goat `windup=.8` locks `chargeDirX/Y`, then `charge=.55` moves at285px/s with a headbutt. Rooster `windup=.85` remains stationary, then `crow=.7` plays sound `rooster` and emits one expanding shockwave, never a charge. `s.shockwaves` is capped12; entries `{x,y,r,prevR,maxR:180,life,maxlife:1,width:12,damage,hitPlayer,hitGirl,ownerId}`. Width is the FULL annular band. Damage tests relative swept radial motion; it is not a filled disc. Charm and Mode avion cancel hostile windups and owned waves. Renderer draws a goat corridor, rooster maximum reach warning, then the real ring and COCORICO text.

Content adds16STUNT_BARKS,8STUNT_REPLIES,16RIVAL_BARKS and separate rival/companion reply pools. Rival taunts are gated on nearby living male rivals, spaced by at least10s. UI shows the matching rival atlas portrait in a small canvas. AudioManager also exposes `setIntensity(0..1)` and `setDucked(bool)`; battle score is32bars at150BPM and menu8bars at104BPM. Independent cursors survive pause/mute; upgrade and in-game dialogue keep the battle score, ducking only the music bus. Global voice cap32; routine effects are throttled to preserve lead and bass.

## V1.4 — approved Toma model
The approved pixel mockup replaces the active v3 torso/arm/gun rig. `hero-model.js` registers five illustrated upper views and three mirrors, with painted hip, muzzle and hand locations. Each upper view contains the head, torso, arms, hands and rifle as one unit; legs animate independently from illustrated pieces and blend into the approved split lower body. `computeHeroPose(s).art` carries the exact upper drawing transform. `solveHeroAim(s,target)` evaluates registered views analytically and returns `{aimX,aimY,aimArtView}`; engine keeps the chosen view locked to avoid an iterative seam flip. The projectile origin is the transformed painted muzzle. Near contact targets under the barrel are hit directly at the normal cadence/damage without firing backwards. UI uses the approved pixel SVG portrait.

`ROOSTER_REACTION` is the explicit Toma reply to a real crow: « Men, farmes ta criss de gueule ! ». `reactToCrow()` runs after the crow sound/wave trigger, merges simultaneous cries within0.9s and delays already queued replies long enough to show the reaction. A cancelled warning never triggers it.
