# Charles, l’Illuminé

Charles is an optional encounter after the existing twenty-minute survival victory. The results retain endless mode and the menu. Meeting Charles requires a second, explicit confirmation explaining that he is invincible and that his two attacks knock Toma out in one hit. Leaving through pause or being hit returns to the victory result; the survival score, kills, coins and rewards remain earned.

## Encounter

Charles uses five original complete-body paintings adapted from the approved illustration and transformation storyboard: levitation, descent, clenched-fist bend, open-mouth scream, empowered ascent. His purple/ivory/gold costume, cape, halo, eye emblem, brown hair/beard and emerald eyes remain visible at gameplay scale. Toma retains the existing whole-body atlas unchanged.

Charles sees through Toma’s AI photographs. Eight sequential lines cover Toma’s claims of superior intelligence and Charles’s replies about the impossible car wheels, fingers and fabricated evidence. Optional chatter follows the existing preference. Essential warning instructions also remain in a persistent HUD banner.

- Truth wave: 2.6-second warning, then a moving gold annulus with a green safe opening. The opening, body margin and swept collision use the same geometry. Only touching the dangerous ring is lethal; its inner ground is safe.
- Cape blade: 2.2-second warning, then a .85-second spin. Leave the outlined circle. The stiff ivory/gold cape sweep stays visibly attached behind Charles’s torso; the ground circle marks the lethal footprint.
- After eighteen registered hits and two completed attacks, Charles descends, bends with fists clenched, screams, then ascends in a localized golden beam/burst. He never loses health. Intro hits do not advance the transformation counter.
- Phase two expands wave range from 300 to 380 pixels and cape radius from 112 to 145 while preserving full warning time.
- The wave breaks a deterministic subset of trees and wooden street props. Charles’s landing then clears nearby eligible scenery within 145 pixels, and his takeoff clears a wider 240-pixel area. Both impacts are unique to Charles and share a maximum of 64 destroyed props. Their stumps remain non-solid after save/reload and into endless mode.

The survival timer stays frozen during the epilogue. Charles has a separate clock. Pausing freezes both. Existing temporary trophy and discovery bonuses expire normally; new discoveries, ordinary enemies and loot cannot interrupt the encounter. Pending trophy choices remain available when continuing endless.

## Safety, resources and persistence

No full-screen flash or strobe. Reduced effects retain the complete warning and hit geometry while removing motion and decorative rays. The transformation beam has a layered ivory/gold core and irregular, branching white-hot electrical arcs with blue/cyan glow that builds through the crouch and scream. Its local light changes smoothly without randomized blinking. Reduced effects retain two static, subdued electrical paths. Arrays retain existing caps; destroyed scenery is bounded to 64 IDs.

Save schema 10 stores the exact attack/pose timer, safe opening, hit count, dialogue index and destroyed prop IDs. Genuine v9 trophy state migrates unchanged. Earlier schemas cannot create a Charles encounter. Invalid phase combinations, health, dimensions, timers, IDs and oversized destruction lists are rejected.

The user approved the final electric transformation preview. Independent managed Chrome QA verified the actual encounter, including final natural-entry framing at 320×568 and 390×844. The final runtime passes 721 automated tests. Physical iPhone/Android/Safari performance and touch behavior remain unverified; publication is tracked separately.
